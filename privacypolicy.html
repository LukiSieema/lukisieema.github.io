Privacy Policy for Idle Survivor: Roguelike RPG

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Idle Survivor: Roguelike RPG ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information when you download and play the Game. We built the Game to work almost entirely on your device, and we've written this policy to reflect that as plainly as possible.

1. Information We Collect

Game progress and settings. Your account level, currencies, equipment, unlocked content, chosen character class, character name, and settings (language, sound, etc.) are stored locally on your device. This information is never transmitted to us, and we have no server that receives or stores it. If you uninstall the Game or clear its data, the local copy is permanently deleted.

Google Play Games Services (optional). If you connect a Google account, the Game uses Google Play Games Services to sign you in, back up your progress to the cloud so you can continue on another device, and submit your scores to leaderboards. The Game tries to sign you in automatically each time it starts if you already have a connected Google account, and you can also sign in manually from Settings; the Game has no in-app sign-out, so to stop this you would manage this app's access from your Google Account settings. Signing in shares your Google Play Games player ID with Google; submitting a score shares that score and your Google Play Games display name, which may be shown publicly to other players on the relevant leaderboard (the Game does not request or display your Play Games profile picture). This is carried out by Google, not by us directly — see Section 3.

Advertising data. The Game displays ads (banner and rewarded-video ads) using Google AdMob. To serve and measure these ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as your advertising ID, device information, IP address, and general app usage related to ad delivery. This collection is carried out by Google, not by us directly — see Section 3 below for details and your controls.

Advertising attribution data. If you discovered the Game through a TikTok ad campaign, the Game uses the TikTok Business SDK to let us see that the campaign led to a real install and app open, using information such as your advertising ID and general device and app-usage information. Like the AdMob data above, this is only sent once you have completed the consent process described in Section 3, and is carried out by TikTok, not by us directly.

In-app purchases (if enabled). If and when the Game offers in-app purchases, they are processed entirely through Google Play's billing system. We never see, collect, or store your payment card details or any other payment information.

Notifications. The Game may schedule local reminder notifications (e.g. daily reward reminders) directly on your device. These are generated on-device and do not involve sending any data to us or to a third party.

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other directly identifying information.

2. How We Use Information

  • To let the Game save and restore your progress on your own device.
  • To back up and sync your progress, and to operate leaderboards, through Google Play Games Services, if you choose to connect a Google account.
  • To display and measure advertising, through Google AdMob, which helps keep the Game free to play.
  • To measure the performance of our TikTok ad campaigns, through the TikTok Business SDK.
  • To send you optional local reminders about the Game, if enabled in your device notification settings.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use any information collected through the Game for purposes unrelated to operating and improving it.

4. App Permissions

The Game requests the following device permissions, used only for the purposes below:

  • Internet / Network state — required to load and display ads.
  • Vibration — used for haptic feedback during gameplay (e.g. taking damage, defeating a boss). Purely cosmetic, no data involved.
  • Notifications — used to show optional, locally scheduled reminders about the Game. You can disable these at any time in your device's notification settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Idle Survivor: Roguelike RPG is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below and we will take appropriate action.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your local game progress is retained on your device for as long as the Game remains installed. You can delete it at any time by clearing the Game's data or uninstalling the Game from your device (Settings > Apps > Idle Survivor > Storage > Clear Data, or simply uninstall). If you signed in with Google Play Games, your cloud save, player ID, and leaderboard scores are retained by Google until you delete them via your Google Account; any advertising or attribution data processed by Google or TikTok is subject to their own retention practices and controls, linked in Section 3.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding the information collected through the Game, such as the right to access, correct, or delete it, or to object to certain processing. Since we do not hold any personal data on our own servers, most of these rights are exercised directly on your device (clearing data / uninstalling) or through Google's or TikTok's own privacy controls (Section 3); if you signed in with Google Play Games, that includes managing or deleting that data through your Google Account, since the Game itself has no in-app sign-out. If you have any question about your rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the Game's features or the third-party services it uses change. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page whenever we make changes. Continued use of the Game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
